# 🕹️ Mini Game Arcade

A fast, responsive, curated browser-based arcade website built with pure **HTML5**, **CSS3**, and **modern JavaScript (ES6+)**.

Live production link: **[https://blade-slicer-game.vercel.app](https://blade-slicer-game.vercel.app)**

---

## 🎮 Playable Games in the Arcade (30 Verified Games)

### Category A: Arcade & Action Games
1. **🍉 Fruit Blade (`/games/fruit-blade/`)**
   - High-speed arcade fruit slicing action with realistic gravity trajectories, 3D slicing physics, juicy color-matched particle bursts, combo multipliers, and explosive bomb hazards.
   - **Controls:** Click & drag with mouse on desktop, or swipe with touch on mobile/tablet.

2. **⚡ Cyber Dash (`/games/cyber-dash/`)**
   - High-reflex neon runner dodging high-voltage plasma barriers, steering across lanes, and collecting glowing energy orbs.
   - **Controls:** Arrow Keys / `A` & `D` on desktop, or tap left/right halves of screen on touchscreens.

3. **🚀 Space Dodge (`/games/space-dodge/`)**
   - 60 FPS HTML5 Canvas cosmic survival arcade. Pilot your spaceship in all directions, evade asteroid swarms, laser sweeps, and space mines, trigger Energy Pulse abilities, and collect shields & speed boosts.
   - **Controls:** Arrow Keys / `WASD` & Spacebar on desktop, touch-and-drag or Virtual Joystick on mobile/tablet.

4. **🗡️ Knife Hit (`/games/knife-hit/`)**
   - Throw virtual knives into spinning wooden targets, avoid embedded blades, slice bonus apples, shatter targets, and defeat intense boss logs.
   - **Controls:** Tap or Spacebar to throw knife upward.

5. **🏀 Ball Jump (`/games/ball-jump/`)**
   - Vertical platform jumper. Bounce up moving, fragile, and spring platforms, collect purple gems, and climb to stratosphere altitudes with screen wrapping.
   - **Controls:** Arrow Keys / `A` & `D` or pointer drag to steer.

6. **🐍 Snake Retro (`/games/snake-retro/`)**
   - The timeless arcade serpent rebuilt with vibrant neon aesthetics. Devour glowing apples, chase golden bonuses, and grow to record lengths without hitting walls or your tail.
   - **Controls:** Arrow Keys / `WASD`, touch swipe, or on-screen D-Pad.

7. **🏓 Pong Classic (`/games/pong-classic/`)**
   - Cyber table tennis with curve-ball spin physics! Rally against adaptive AI with escalating ball speed or battle 2-player local.
   - **Controls:** `W`/`S` and `Up`/`Down`, or drag left/right screen halves.

8. **🚁 Flappy Copter (`/games/flappy-copter/`)**
   - Cyber gyro-copter flight navigation. Thrust through high-voltage neon laser gates, earn Bronze to Platinum medals, and set flight records.
   - **Controls:** Tap, Click, or Spacebar to thrust.

9. **👾 Space Shooter (`/games/space-shooter/`)**
   - Top-down galactic invader combat. Blast alien drone squadrons and cruisers, collect Triple Lasers and Shields, and conquer Dreadnought flagships.
   - **Controls:** `WASD` / Arrow Keys or touch drag; auto-firing continuous lasers.

### Category B: Puzzle & Brain Games
10. **🧠 Memory Match (`/games/memory-match/`)**
    - Brain-training arcade card-matching game featuring 3D flip card animations, live elapsed timer, move counter, match audio synthesis, win state overlay, and persistent best-record tracking.
    - **Controls:** Mouse click, touch tap, or keyboard navigation (`Tab` + `Enter`/`Space`).

11. **🧩 Neon Block Puzzle (`/games/block-puzzle/`)**
    - 10×10 strategic block placement puzzle with 24+ polyomino shapes, simultaneous multi-line clears, combo multipliers, 3 undo moves, and a 3×3 zone bomb clearance tool.
    - **Controls:** Mouse drag-and-drop or tap-to-select then tap-to-place; `Z` to undo, `P` to pause.

12. **🔢 Neon Number Merge (`/games/number-merge/`)**
    - 2048-inspired numerical slide and merge puzzle on a 4×4 grid. Features 4 modes (Classic, Time Attack 90s, Endless, Challenge 512 in 60 moves), 3 difficulty tiers (Target 1024, 2048, 4096), Undo, AI Hint solver, and board Shuffle power-ups.
    - **Controls:** Arrow Keys / `WASD` on desktop, 4-direction touch swipe or on-screen Virtual D-Pad on mobile.

13. **❌ Tic Tac Toe (`/games/tic-tac-toe/`)**
    - Cyber neon 3x3 tactical battleground. Test your wits against an unbeatable Minimax algorithm AI, Casual AI, or play head-to-head with a friend.
    - **Controls:** Tap or click empty cell.

14. **🔴 Connect Four (`/games/connect-four/`)**
    - Vertical disc dropping strategy on a 7x6 grid. Connect 4 discs horizontally, vertically, or diagonally against smart lookahead AI or a friend.
    - **Controls:** Tap column buttons to drop disc.

15. **💣 Minesweeper (`/games/minesweeper/`)**
    - Demine cyber terrain without detonating explosives. Features 100% first-click safety, cascading empty spaces, number clues, and mobile touch flag toggle.
    - **Controls:** Tap to uncover, Right-click or Flag mode to mark danger.

16. **🧩 Sliding Puzzle (`/games/sliding-puzzle/`)**
    - Classic 15-Puzzle & 8-Puzzle tile slider. Reorder numbered tiles sequentially in minimal moves. 100% solvable guaranteed shuffle!
    - **Controls:** Tap tiles neighboring the empty space to slide them.

17. **🧮 Math Challenge (`/games/math-challenge/`)**
    - Speed mental arithmetic blitz in 45 seconds! Solve rapid calculation equations, chain combo multipliers, and sharpen cognitive speed.
    - **Controls:** Tap the correct number choice among 4 options.

18. **🎨 Color Fill (`/games/color-fill/`)**
    - Chromatic flood fill puzzle! Absorb neighboring colored blocks from top-left to unify the entire 144-tile board in 22 moves.
    - **Controls:** Tap palette colors to flood your territory.

19. **🔢 Sudoku Master (`/games/sudoku-master/`)**
    - Classic 9x9 Japanese numerical logic deduction. Features conflict error detection, matching digit highlighting, virtual numpad, and 3 difficulty tiers.
    - **Controls:** Click cell then tap numpad (1-9) or use keyboard digits.

20. **🔍 Word Search (`/games/word-search/`)**
    - Themed Arcade & Cyber word hunt! Drag across the letter matrix horizontally, vertically, or diagonally to complete your target word bank.
    - **Controls:** Drag or tap start and end letters to highlight words.

## 🚀 Deployment

Deployable instantly to Vercel as a 100% static project:
```bash
vercel --prod
```
All assets are self-contained with procedural audio and SVG/Canvas graphics, requiring zero external CDNs or backend servers.
