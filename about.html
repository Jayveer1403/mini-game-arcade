ABOUT OUR ARCADE

Independent, high-performance browser gaming built with modern web standards.

🚀 Who We Are & Our Mission

Mini Game Arcade is an independent web gaming platform founded in 2026. Our vision is to bring back the golden age of instant, accessible arcade entertainment—free from massive 50GB downloads, invasive login walls, pay-to-win microtransactions, and aggressive ad trackers.

We believe that the modern open web (HTML5, Canvas, Web Audio API, and CSS3) is capable of delivering desktop-caliber 60 FPS gameplay natively inside any browser on phones, tablets, laptops, and workstations.

🎮 100% Original Content & Native Engineering

Unlike aggregator websites that embed third-party iframes filled with slow malware or stolen Flash conversions, every single game in our catalog is engineered in-house.

Our Technical Standards:

  • Zero Bloatware: No bloated engines or external runtime dependencies. Games boot in milliseconds.
  • Procedural Web Audio: All music and sound effects (swipes, chimes, explosions) are synthesized directly in code via the browser's AudioContext.
  • Vector & Canvas Graphics: Original vector SVG artwork and GPU-accelerated 2D Canvas rendering that sharpens crisply at 4K resolution.
  • Universal Accessibility: Touch gestures, virtual joysticks, keyboard keys (WASD/Arrows), and screen-reader compliant metadata.
