PRIVACY POLICY

Effective Date: November 1, 2026

At Mini Game Arcade (accessible from https://online-games-arcade.vercel.app), the privacy of our visitors is of paramount importance to us. This Privacy Policy outlines the types of information collected and recorded by Mini Game Arcade and how we use it.

1. Information We Collect

Mini Game Arcade is designed as an instantaneous web gaming portal. We do not require account creation, registration, email sign-ups, or login credentials to enjoy any of our 30 games.

  • Personal Information: We do not ask for or collect personally identifiable information (PII) such as your real name, physical address, or phone number.
  • Server Log Files: Like standard hosting providers, our hosting platform (Vercel) automatically logs technical requests, including IP addresses, browser user-agents, referral URLs, and timestamps to ensure uptime, network security, and DDoS protection.

2. Browser LocalStorage & Game Data

Our games store persistent high scores, level unlock progress, and audio mute preferences locally on your machine using HTML5 localStorage.

This information is stored strictly within your browser client and is never transmitted to, collected by, or stored on our servers. You may clear your browser's site data at any time to delete all local records.

3. Google AdSense & Third-Party Advertising Technologies

Mini Game Arcade works with third-party advertising vendors, including Google AdSense, to serve advertisements when you visit our website.

  • Google, as a third-party vendor, uses cookies (including the DoubleClick cookie) to serve ads based on your visit to this site and other websites on the Internet.
  • Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.
  • Alternatively, you can opt out of third-party vendor cookies for personalized advertising by visiting www.aboutads.info.

4. Non-Intrusive Ad Experience Commitment

We strictly comply with Google AdSense quality guidelines and the Coalition for Better Ads standards:

  • We never place deceptive ad blocks that mimic game buttons or interface controls.
  • We never display ads that block the active game Canvas or encourage accidental clicks.
  • We never deploy pop-unders, auto-redirecting URLs, or full-screen unskippable ad walls.

5. Children's Online Privacy Protection (COPPA)

Mini Game Arcade does not knowingly collect any Personal Identifiable Information from children under the age of 13. If a parent or guardian believes that Mini Game Arcade has personal information of a child under the age of 13 in its database, please contact us immediately, and we will use our best efforts to promptly remove such information from our records.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions or require more information about our Privacy Policy, please feel free to reach out to us at support@online-games-arcade.vercel.app or via our Contact Page.
